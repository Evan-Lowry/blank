Assessment Bank

About

blank. turns a Crowdmark results link into a blank copy of the assessment, and keeps a public bank of past tests, quizzes, and assignments for Waterloo courses.

Crowdmark keeps the original blank pages for every assessment. blank. fetches those pages and rebuilds them as a PDF. It never uses your answers or marks. The only things saved are the blank copy and its course, term, and type. Your link, grades, and any student work are not stored.

Anything you convert is added to the public bank, so only use assessments you have permission to share. If something in the bank looks wrong, use the ⋯ menu on its row and choose Report a problem.

Convert from Crowdmark in one click

Drag this button to your bookmarks bar. Open your shareable Crowdmark results link, click the bookmark, and blank. opens with the link filled in. You still press Prepare PDF to confirm, since converting adds the blank copy to the public bank.

Get blank copy

Your lists are saved in your browser. If you turn on sync, your list names and the assessments in them are also saved on the server under a private code, and nothing else.

blank. is a student project. It isn't affiliated with or endorsed by the University of Waterloo or Crowdmark. The source is on GitHub.
